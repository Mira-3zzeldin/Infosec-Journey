## LCD Panel Types

|Type|Viewing Angle|Color Accuracy|Lag|Best For|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|TN|Poor|Poor|Lowest|Competitive gaming (up to 240 Hz)|
|IPS|Best|Best|Slightly more than TN|Graphic design, video editing, vertical mounting|
|VA|Good|Good|Slight (ms)|All-around / jack-of-all-trades|
|OLED|Best|Excellent|Low|Premium displays; risk: burn-in|
|Mini-LED|Good|Good|—|Bright-room contrast, cheaper than OLED|

## Display Attributes

|Attribute|Definition|Unit / Range|
|---|---|---|
|Refresh Rate|How often the screen redraws|Hz (60–480 Hz typical)|

|Mouse Optical Sensor|Fails on transparent/glass surfaces (no reflectivity)|
